clip-ops privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026.

clip-ops is a private tool used only by the owner of the Basic Brindle Clips channels (@basicbrindle62) to upload its own short videos and read its own channel statistics. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's Google sign-in it can upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and read that channel's public video data and YouTube Analytics reports (views, likes, comments, average view duration and percentage).

What it stores

Only per-video counts and averages for the owner's own videos, kept in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data about viewers or any other person is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Revoking access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/basicbrindleclips/clip-media.
